COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITIES

A defined problem.
A valuable outcome.

Multiverse explores well-defined software technology opportunities: acquiring suitable proprietary components, separately scoped development, or carefully agreed licensing. The goal is a clear outcome and bounded responsibilities—not an indefinite staffing arrangement.

See existing products and prototypes →
01 / EXISTING ASSETS

Acquire existing technology

Discuss acquiring rights to technology or a component where the scope, ownership and transferability are clear. Public MIT-licensed code, such as SWAAG, is already freely licensed and cannot be presented as an exclusive acquisition.

02 / FIXED SCOPE

Focused implementation

Apply or adapt established technology to a specific problem with clearly defined deliverables and acceptance criteria.

03 / SELECTED PARTNERSHIPS

Technology arrangements

Technology transfer or carefully scoped licensing where commercial responsibilities, ownership and ongoing obligations are explicit.

HOW TO START

Lead with the problem.
Not the hours.

01 Describe the operational challenge and current workaround.

02 Quantify its impact if possible: cost, delay, risk, or lost opportunity.

03 Share your desired outcome, budget range, and timeline.

04 Explore a suitable technology approach and bounded commercial scope.
