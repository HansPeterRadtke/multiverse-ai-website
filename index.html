01 / PUBLISHED SOFTWARE PRODUCT

Paid orders are waiting to ship.
Which need follow-up first?

Late Order Monitor is an application for Wix Stores. It shows paid orders that still need fulfilment, helps filter them by age, and exports a list for follow-up. The app does not change or fulfil orders.
