01 / PUBLISHED SOFTWARE · FOR WIX STORE OWNERS

Late Order Monitor — an app for managing existing shop orders.

For businesses that operate an online shop using Wix Stores. When customers place orders in that shop, the owner needs to know which paid orders have not been shipped yet. This separate, read-only app displays those orders, helps identify older ones, and lets the owner export a follow-up list.

Important: Multiverse does not take customer orders here. The screenshot below is from a sample Wix shop's order dashboard, not a Multiverse checkout.

02 / AI PROJECT · DOCUMENT ANSWERS WITH SOURCES

KnowMoreDiRT — ask questions about your documents.

Imagine you have many folders of reports, notes, logs and transcripts. Instead of opening files one by one, the goal is to point KnowMoreDiRT at a folder, ask a question in everyday language, and receive a short answer. The system keeps source evidence internally so results can be investigated rather than blindly trusted.

So far, this page shows evidence from a limited test using fictional documents. It is not a production-ready search service or an AI system running new queries directly on this website.

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE · NOT FOR SALE AS A FINISHED APP

What has actually been tested?

A controlled one-file experiment returned the expected answer and identified the supporting text. The system used local models and cached intermediate results. That does not establish general performance across real company document collections.
