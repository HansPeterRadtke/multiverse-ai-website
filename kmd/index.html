HOW KNOWMOREDIRT WORKS

From evidence to meaning.

01

Read arbitrary folders

Scan readable files and preserve natural file metadata, source identity and text spans.

02

Build discourse structure

Store referents, contexts, conditions and grounded relations in a persistent SQLite discourse graph.

03

Ask grounded questions

Use local models for bounded language interpretation and deterministic checks against source evidence.

Research boundary. KMD is an active engineering project. Cross-document identity resolution, broader accuracy, deployment readiness and the complete knowledge-library pipeline remain under development. The diagrams explain the architecture and goals; they do not certify universal reasoning accuracy.
