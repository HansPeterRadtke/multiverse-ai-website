# KnowMoreDiRT — source-grounded AI knowledge demonstration

Target: about 75–90 seconds plus an 8–15-second muted homepage excerpt. Status: **script and shot plan only**; real KMD footage has not yet been captured under this campaign.

**Business promise (qualified):** make large collections of technical documents more navigable and answers verifiable by linking claims back to original source material. This does not claim every answer is perfect or all document types are supported. Do not advertise proprietary document access from public examples.
