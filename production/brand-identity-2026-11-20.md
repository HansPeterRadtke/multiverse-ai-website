# Multiverse brand identity and social-sharing assets — November 20, 2026

The existing public mark is a geometric teal M with a small upper-right dot on a dark-navy background. It has been retained, not replaced with a speculative new identity. Assets are derived from the existing public SVG favicon and are original artwork rather than simulated SWAAG or KMD screenshots.

- `assets/brand/multiverse-lockup.svg`: scalable business wordmark with the original M geometry.
- `assets/brand/multiverse-profile-icon.png`: square account/avatar artwork. Do not mistake this for a photograph of the owner.
- `assets/brand/multiverse-linkedin-banner.png`: 1584 x 396 suggested LinkedIn profile background, with critical text on the right to avoid the usual personal-avatar overlay. It is **prepared, not applied to LinkedIn**.
- `assets/brand/multiverse-social-preview.png`: 1200 x 630 shared website social-preview illustration. Page-specific original text descriptions, canonicals, and URLs remain intact.

`python3 scripts/generate_brand_assets.py` regenerates the images; it needs Pillow and DejaVu Sans. `python3 scripts/refresh_public_seo.py` adds the shared preview metadata, while retaining the actual Blender render preview on the three-dimensional production page. No crawler or social platform is guaranteed to refresh its cached image immediately.
